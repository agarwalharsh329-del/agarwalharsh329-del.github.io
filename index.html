Red & Unforgettable

Chapter 6

Red suits you too well. This one honestly feels like a favourite.

I wish I had treated your birthday with the same care and excitement that I feel when I see you like this. I’m sorry for that day, and for all the little mistakes that followed — but I’m endlessly thankful that, somehow, you’re still on my side.

So I want to ask you something, pineapple 🍍 — gently, not with any pressure:
if you still feel like celebrating your birthday with me, even in a small and simple way, I would be really happy to be there with you.

And if you don’t, I will still respect your feelings completely. You matter more than any plan or party.

“Whatever you choose, thank you for staying in my story this far.”
